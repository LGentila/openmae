And from your iPhone.

The iPhone and iPad app becomes your Mac’s microphone, speaker and screen. Talk to Maé from anywhere: the work stays on the Mac.

  • One code to scan, onceYour Mac shows a code, the app scans it. Done.
  • At home, directlyOn the same Wi-Fi, the iPhone talks straight to your Mac.
  • Elsewhere, through an encrypted relayThe relay only sees encrypted packets: never a word, never a sound. You can also host your own.

What you need.

A recent Mac is enough. The more memory it has, the larger the language model.

A Mac with Apple silicon

  • Apple silicon: M1 or later.
  • macOS 15 or later.
  • At least 8 GB of memory.
  • 5.7 to 30.1 GB free for the models, depending on the Mac’s memory.
  • For the iPhone and iPad app: iOS 18 or later.

Questions.

When can I install it?

There’s no date yet. OpenMaé is in development, and there’s no download yet. To try it now, you build the app from its source code: everything is explained on GitHub.

Does Maé work offline?

Yes. You need the internet once, to download the models. After that she works offline, except for the features that go online, if you turn them on.

Who hears what I say?

Your Mac, and only your Mac. The microphone’s sound is processed on your Mac and sent nowhere. With the iPhone app, it travels encrypted to your Mac.

Which languages does she speak?

French first, and English. She answers in the language you speak to her. The app itself is in French and English.

Can my Mac run it?

You need a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later), macOS 15 or later and at least 8 GB of memory. Intel Macs are not supported.
