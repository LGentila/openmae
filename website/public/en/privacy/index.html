What stays on your Mac, and what leaves it.

OpenMaé is made so that your voice stays at home. Here is, feature by feature, what stays on your Mac and what goes online.

Always on your Mac

  • Your voice. The microphone’s sound is processed on your Mac and sent nowhere.
  • The models. Speech recognition, the language model and Maé’s voice all run on your Mac.
  • Memory and history, if you turn them on, stay in a folder on your Mac: never online, never in iCloud. Settings show what Maé remembers and can erase everything.
  • No account, no telemetry. OpenMaé sends no usage statistics.

Once: downloading the models

At first launch, OpenMaé downloads its models from Hugging Face, from 5.7 to 30.1 GB depending on your Mac’s memory. Every file is checked before it is used: these are exactly the versions that were tested. After that, Maé works without the internet.

Only if you turn it on

Everything below stays off until you turn it on in Settings.

Web search

What leaves
The few keywords of the search.
To
Brave Search, with your own key, or your own SearXNG server.
Good to know
The results are not kept: they only live in the conversation.

Weather, rates and travel times

What leaves
The place, currency or crypto you ask about. If you say “here”, your Mac’s location, which macOS asks you for once.
To
Open-Meteo for the weather, Frankfurter for the European Central Bank’s rates, CoinGecko for cryptos, Apple Maps for travel times.

Events, reminders, timers and shortcuts

What leaves
Nothing from OpenMaé. macOS syncs your calendars and reminders as usual, and your shortcuts do what you taught them.

Long tasks handed to Claude Code

What leaves
The task you hand over, and what Claude Code needs to do it.
To
Claude Code, installed on your Mac with your account, which works with Anthropic’s online models.

iPhone and iPad

What leaves
Your voice and Maé’s answers, encrypted end to end between your device and your Mac.
Through
Your Wi-Fi, directly, at home. Elsewhere, an OpenMaé relay: the project’s, or your own.
Good to know
The relay sees IP addresses and how much data goes through. Never a word, never a sound.

Received information

What arrives
The sentences your systems, like Home Assistant or a script, send to Maé.
Through
The home relay, on a machine at your place, or a relay on the internet, which cannot read what goes through.
Good to know
These sentences only inform her: they never trigger an action.

This website

  • This website counts its visits with Umami, installed on a server of the project, without cookies. For each page view, it records the page, the site you came from, your country and city, your browser and its language, your system and your screen size. Your country and city are worked out from your IP address, which it does not record. These figures only serve to know how many people come here.
  • Its fonts come from the site itself.
  • The QR code that links your iPhone to your Mac opens an address on this site, openmae.com/p. Your Mac’s key and the one-time code come after the # sign: your browser never sends that part of the address to the site. This page does not count visits.

Check for yourself

OpenMaé’s code is public: everything written here can be checked on GitHub. A question? Ask it on GitHub.
