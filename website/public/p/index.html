Open this code in the OpenMaé app.

This code links your iPhone or iPad to your Mac. It opens in the OpenMaé app, not in the browser.

  1. Open the OpenMaé app on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Scan the code shown on your Mac with it.
  3. Done: you can talk to Maé from your iPhone.

A code is valid for 5 minutes and serves only once. If it no longer works, show a new one on your Mac: Settings › iPhone and iPad, then “Add a device”.
